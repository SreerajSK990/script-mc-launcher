# Script Minecraft Launcher v0.24.2

Hey everyone, v0.24.2 fixes mouse-wheel scrolling across the launcher.

---

## What is new in this release

- **Scrolling works wherever your mouse is:** Lists like an instance's installed mods used to ignore the mouse wheel unless the pointer was in the empty space beside them. The same thing happened over image cards, icons, cut-off names and tab strips on the Dashboard, Instances, Settings, Mod Browser and Skins pages and in several dialogs. A style rule meant to stop the window from bouncing was blocking every scroll that started over one of those elements. The rule now applies only to the window itself, so the page scrolls from anywhere.

---

## Improvements & Fixes

- The window still doesn't rubber-band or bounce when you reach the top or bottom of a page.
