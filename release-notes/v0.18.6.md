# Script Minecraft Launcher v0.18.6

Hi everyone, v0.18.6 resolves the cape rendering bug in Skin & Cape Studio and embeds genuine, offline-ready official cape textures directly into the native runtime.

---

## What is new in this release

- **Self-Contained Official Cape Textures:** All 14 historic preset capes (including the 15th Anniversary, Cherry Blossom, Migrator, Vanilla, Mojang Studios, Purple Heart, Follower, Minecon 2011 through 2016, Turtle, and Cobalt capes) now embed verified base64 PNG textures directly. They load instantly with zero network requests and work completely offline.
- **Instant Back-Equipment 3D Toggling:** Switching between Cape, Elytra, and None now updates the 3D player mesh immediately without re-fetch latency or canvas texture desync.
- **Smart Cape Preview Initialization:** When entering the Capes studio, the 3D player automatically equips your active cape or defaults to the first available cape preset rather than leaving the model bare.

---

## Improvements & Fixes

- **Fixed 404 Cape Texture Bug:** Resolved the issue where preset capes pointed to outdated external texture hashes that failed with HTTP 404, preventing the 3D viewer and thumbnail cards from displaying cape graphics.
- **Eliminated WebGL Canvas Texture Failures:** Using local base64 textures bypasses external cross-origin restrictions, ensuring Three.js skin and cape rendering succeeds reliably across all platforms.
- **Thumbnail Display Polish:** Historic preset and user library cape cards now render pixel-crisp previews of their textures.
